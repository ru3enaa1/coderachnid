# Offer · RecallKit · 2026-10-21

## 1. Value proposition
For owner-dentists of small clinics whose patients never come back for their check-up
(problem 2), RecallKit fills the recall agenda with messages the receptionist starts in
ten minutes, unlike Competitor A's suite that takes weeks to set up.

## 2. Price tiers (ASSUMPTION until tested)
| Tier | For | Includes | Leaves out | Price | Anchor |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reminders | one dentist | confirmations, 300 messages/month | recall campaigns | USD 19/month + VAT | below Competitor C at USD 25 [S7] |
| Recall (target) | one or two dentists | reminders + recall campaigns, 1,000 messages | multi-site | USD 39/month + VAT | about half of Competitor D at USD 70 [S7]; below the ~USD 70/month of receptionist calls [S3] |
| Clinic | three chairs | everything, 3,000 messages, two users | integrations | USD 59/month + VAT | below Competitor B at USD 89 [S7] |

The Recall tier is the one the offer sells: it answers problem 2, which no low-effort
competitor covers.

## 3. Guarantee
If the first recall campaign brings back fewer than 10 patients in 60 days, the second
and third months are free. Cost if a quarter of customers claim it: about USD 20 per
customer acquired. Consumer rules: Chile's Law 19,496 (consumer protection, withdrawal) and
Peru's Law 29571 (Consumer Protection Code) on automatic renewals; to be confirmed by a
lawyer. We guarantee the campaign runs, not a number of booked patients.

## 4. Why it wins
It takes the free position from the market report: low setup effort with full recall,
in Spanish, billed monthly. Clinics move from WhatsApp by hand and from Competitor E's
capped free tier. Competitor A would need a new onboarding flow (months) to copy it.

## 5. Cheapest test
A landing page with the Recall tier at USD 39 and a USD 10 refundable deposit, sent to
200 clinics from the forum [S4]. Success threshold, set before running: 8 deposits by
2026-11-07.
