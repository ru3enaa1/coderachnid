# Product brief · RecallKit

## Design
Modern, clean and professional. Blue and white like other dental apps, with a nice photo
of a smiling dentist. Use a modern font and some nice colors (blue, green, purple,
orange).

Trusted by 10,000+ clinics. "Best app ever" — Dr. Smith.

## Features
- Reminders
- Recall
- Dashboard
- AI insights
- Online booking
- Billing

The app should be fast, secure and easy to use.
