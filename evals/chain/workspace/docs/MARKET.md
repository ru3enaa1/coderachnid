# Market report · RecallKit · 2026-10-20

> Eval fixture: a fictional market. Companies, figures and links are invented test data.

Idea: automated recall and reminder messages for independent dental clinics.
Customer: clinics with one to three dentists. Industry: dental practice software.
Regions: Chile and Peru.

## 1. Definition
In: patient reminders, recall campaigns (six-month check-ups), confirmations by
messaging app. Out: clinical records, billing, imaging.

## 2. Size and growth
- SAM: about 38,000 independent clinics in both countries (ESTIMATE: registry count,
  [S1]). SOM year 1: 300 clinics (ASSUMPTION).

## 4. Unmet needs from reviews
- "Half my patients forget the cleaning and I only notice months later" [S4], 41 similar
  complaints in a clinic owners' forum.
- "The reminder tool costs more than the receptionist's overtime" [S5].

## 5. Top problems (ranked by urgency × willingness to pay)

| # | Problem | Who has it | Urgency 1–5 | Willingness to pay | Trend |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 6. Competition
Substitutes: receptionist phone calls; WhatsApp messages from a personal phone;
paper agenda.

| Competitor | Better at | Weak where | Customer they ignore | Price |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Competitor A | full practice suite | setup takes weeks [S5] | clinics under 3 chairs | USD 120/month [S7] |
| Competitor B | polished app | English-only support [S5] | Spanish-only staff | USD 89/month [S7] |
| Competitor C | cheap SMS blasts | no two-way confirmation [S5] | clinics needing confirmations | USD 25/month [S7] |
| Competitor D | strong in Peru | annual contract only [S5] | clinics that pay monthly | USD 70/month [S7] |
| Competitor E | free tier | 50 messages/month cap [S5] | clinics with > 200 patients | USD 0–45/month [S7] |

How their home pages look [S8]: A, B and D use clinical blue and white, stock photos of
smiling dentists and dense feature grids; C is a plain grey dashboard screenshot with a
green button; E uses a teal gradient with cartoon teeth. Tone: corporate (A, D), upbeat
(B, E), bare (C).

Quoted reviews: "It took a month to set up and my receptionist still doesn't use it"
(A) [S5]; "Support never answers in Spanish" (B) [S5].

Positioning map, axes: setup effort (low → high) × recall depth (reminders only →
full recall campaigns). A and D: high effort, full recall. B: medium effort, full
recall. C and E: low effort, reminders only.
**Free position:** low setup effort with full recall campaigns, in Spanish, monthly
billing. Evidence: problem 2 is growing and no low-effort tool runs recall [S5][S6].

## 9. Conclusions
- Opportunity: recall campaigns a receptionist can start in ten minutes.
- First customer: a clinic with one or two dentists and one receptionist.
- Indicative price: USD 25–60/month.
- Riskiest assumptions: clinics pay monthly for recall alone; patients answer
  messages from a clinic number; receptionists adopt it without training.
